Optimization review · CodeIsland Buddy (iOS + Watch) · 2026-07-21 · for Greg Harned

Buddy, optimized from here: seven before/after moves

The identity call is made and shipped: Buddy is a coding-agent companion, the 19-module hub is trimmed to 7 keepers behind a reversible flag (commit e69ae32). This review optimizes from that point. The through-line: every screen now has one job, so make each screen do that job in fewer seconds, with the verb in reach, at every text size. Seven before/after pairs, ordered by impact on the one scenario the product exists for: Greg is away from the Mac and an agent needs him, or finished. Nothing here re-opens the trim or the closed items (wordmark at rest, neutral badge rail, Mac light mode).

Grounded in the app as it sits today, not the plan:
  • Full reads of the live iOS shell: CompanionCommandCenterView.swift (Now surface, signal board, timeline, dock), RemoteApprovalView.swift (approval and question cards), PersonalHubView.swift (Tools directory), ContentView.swift (Sessions surface, CITheme, composer), WatchContentView.swift, and Sources/CodeIslandCore/PersonalHubProtocol.swift (catalog :18-40, racks :820-831).

  • Consolidates and extends, does not duplicate: Batch 02 items B2-12 to B2-16 and the forward review's moves are folded in where they touch the phone; the log at docs/product/codeisland-design-review-log.md stays the ledger of record.

Pair 1iOS + Watch + Live ActivityEffort MImpact Very high

The verb travels with the alert

Buddy's core moment is a notification, and today every surface that announces a need sends Greg into the app to act on it. Close the loop where the alert lands: approve, deny, or answer without unlocking.

No UNNotificationCategory registered anywhere; notification taps only launch (CompanionAppDelegate.swift:59-66). Live Activity has .widgetURL deep links, no Button(intent:). Watch quick-actions punts questions: "Answer on iPhone" (WatchContentView.swift:401-408), though WatchQuestionOptions already exists for the nearby transport (:253-303). Consolidates B2-15 and forward move 5.

Blocked-time leads and escalates (calm under 5m, hot after 15m). Safe commands approve in one tap with a 3-second undo instead of a dialog; destructive requests keep the confirm sheet with Deny filled, exactly as shipped. Queue rows carry their own waiting ages, worst first.

Why it fitsThirty approvals a day at two taps each is sixty taps of pure ceremony. Blocked-time is idle agent capacity made visible, which is the actual cost Greg is paying while away.

Pair 7Design systemEffort MImpact HighB2-14, consolidated

Make orange mean one thing: an accent token, a type ramp, a radius scale

CITheme has no accent token, so the attention color moonlights as the brand: the same orange says "approval needed", "capture a note", "join a meeting", and "connect", while submitting an answer runs two nearly identical ad hoc blues. When orange is everywhere, orange is nothing.

CITheme defines bg/surface/fg only (ContentView.swift:2264-2285); its own comment says "Signal color belongs to the current action", yet no signal token exists. HubTheme.accent = system orange (PersonalHubView.swift:8). Submit blue #61ADFF (QuestionOptionsView) vs question-tile blue #579EFF (CompanionCommandCenterView.swift:373). Measured sprawl: 21 distinct type sizes, 166 call sites; 16 distinct radii, 5 to 30. The Mac got NotchTokens in Batch 01; the phone and Watch got nothing.
